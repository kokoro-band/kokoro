The current GLBs, thumbnails and oak floor finish were created for Kokoro in Blender.
The twelve additions for bedrooms, storage, dining and workspaces are original Kokoro designs.
The historical mappings below apply only to the eight entries in the initial catalog.
The initial catalog used models from the Kenney Furniture Kit (2.0).
https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
